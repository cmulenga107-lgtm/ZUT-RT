ZAMBIA UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY
RESULTS TRACKER

A modern platform that tracks examination results processing and notifies students instantly.

project

1. IDENTIFIED PROBLEM

* The problem occurs during the examination results-processing and release process at ZUT.

* Students, Lecturers, Examination officers and administrators are affected.

* After examinations, students may wait a long time while marks are submitted, verified, compiled and approved.

The delay creates uncertainity and can affect academic planning, registration and personal arrangements for students, especially those from distant places like Livingstone.

2. PROPOSED SOFTWARE SOLUTION

System Name: ZAMBIA UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY RESULTS TRACKER (ZUT-RT).

Users: Students, Lecturers, Examination officers and academic administrators.

The system improves communication by showing progress and notifying students when results are released.

3. What ZUT-RT does

Track every result from submission to release, and get told the moment yours is ready.

Live tracking

See the state of every result at a glance, using three simple colours.

Done Green
In progress Orange
Pending Grey

Instant alert

Get an SMS and an email as soon as your results are out.

Student dashboard

View your status and history in one place.

Secure access

Role-based login keeps students and staff to what they need.

Progress reports

Admins track each department's progress.

4. BENEFITS OF ZUT-RT

Clear Progress Updates

Students can see whether results are pending, being processed, or released, reducing uncertainty while they wait.

Fewer Status Inquiries

Visible progress can reduce repeated requests to lecturers and examination staff for updates.

Timely Notifications

Students can be notified when results are released instead of checking the system repeatedly.

Better Academic Planning

Knowing the results status helps students plan registration and their next academic steps.

Easier Staff Follow-up

Progress reports help administrators identify processing stages or departments that need attention.
